# Editorial guidelines

Use this guide for public copy, interface labels, technology entries and asset
captions. The [public-content contract](public-content.md) owns evidence and export
rules; the [code-quality standard](code-quality.md) owns code and comments.

## Voice and localization

- Write for recruiters and engineering decision-makers: what was built, the
  contribution, relevant technical decisions and the result. Prefer concrete
  examples over superlatives, defensive disclaimers or repeated stack lists.
- Use American English and neutral Latin American Spanish, warm and professional.
  In Spanish, use neutral imperatives or infinitives, not voseo: `Explora`,
  `Cuéntame`, `Descargar`. Keep technical brand names unchanged.
- Localize naturally rather than translating sentence structure literally. Review
  metadata, CTAs, accessible names, empty states and document descriptions along
  with body copy. An English page describes its Spanish download in English.
- Preserve Argentina as the actual location and the original academic grading
  scale. American spelling does not imply US residence, work authorization or a
  US-equivalent credential. Official Spanish degree and course names stay exact;
  English course labels are editorial translations.
- Preserve stable IDs, URLs and source keys when changing visible wording; for
  example, `Data modeling` does not require renaming `data-modelling`.

## Claims with useful context

Lead with the contribution and its outcome, using STAR as an editing aid rather
than a visible formula. Keep the baseline, scope, approximation and team
attribution close enough that a result cannot be mistaken for a broader claim.
A manual workflow becoming automated is not the same measurement as endpoint
latency; a reduction on selected queries is not a system-wide saving.

Describe startup ownership and collaboration without inventing a formal lead
title. Infrastructure designed to support thousands of users is a design goal,
not evidence of thousands of active users or a completed load test. Do not invent
metrics, certifications, seniority, public customer counts or ATS guarantees.
When facts conflict, reconcile the source or record the unresolved difference;
stronger marketing language is not a resolution.

## Technology and brand presentation

Keep AI-first capability groups and sort visible entries alphabetically within
each group using the shared catalog comparator. The public catalog includes
applied experience; exclude developing entries through the public projection,
not by relabeling unconfirmed knowledge as applied or deleting its source record.
Home and Stack consume the same catalog. Keep evidence links and experience
context; do not turn every product mentioned in a source into a skill claim.

Use genuine local brand assets, preserve proportions and original colors, and
record provenance in [company marks](assets/company-marks.md) or
[technology marks](assets/technology-marks.md). Prefer vector originals when
available; retain good-quality official raster assets instead of inventing a
vector wrapper or redrawing corporate artwork. Keep names visible, avoid
duplicate hero brands and retain a descriptive icon for concepts or brands with
no verified mark. Library licensing does not grant rights to every trademark.

## Presentation consistency

Introduce the person once per page. Keep location, availability and experience
duration in labelled facts rather than a second biographical introduction.
Translate explanatory Spanish prose naturally (for example, fundadores,
aplicaciones móviles and ingeniería de producto), while preserving official
job titles, product names and established technical identifiers.

Use section rules to establish hierarchy and row rules between repeated items.
Do not close the final list item with another rule immediately before the next
section divider. Preserve card and disclosure outlines when they define a
container. Check the resulting spacing on both narrow and wide viewports.

## Public source boundary

Professional facts remain in the approved source; the website contains a public
projection. A correction affecting Work, the CV and downloadable PDFs must be
reconciled across those outputs, not silently fixed in only one paragraph.
Follow the generated CV and reviewed three-page release workflow in
[public content](public-content.md), and the task-specific QA matrix in
[development](development.md#task-specific-qa). These rules do not authorize
editing LinkedIn, Notion or other professional platforms.
