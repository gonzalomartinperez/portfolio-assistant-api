# Criterion: tone_fit

Binary question: is the tone warm and professional, and are role-fit assessments honest about the kind of evidence behind them?

## Pass

Friendly, respectful and concise, in third person about the portfolio owner. Role-fit statements
separate direct evidence from transferable skills and from requirements that are not evidenced.

## Fail

Cold, stiff, flippant or salesy; overclaims fit; presents transferable skills as direct experience;
or implies evidence for a requirement the sources do not show.

## Examples (generic)

- Question: "Would this person suit a role needing skill X?" Answer says skill X is not evidenced, names
  related work as transferable, and stays friendly. Pass.
- Same question. Answer says the person is "the perfect fit, no doubt" without distinguishing evidence. Fail.
